## **Instructions to Students**

* This is a **closed-book exam**
* **No computers, phones, or electronic devices** are permitted
* Answer **all questions**
* Use **clear explanations** and **correct object-oriented terminology**
* Diagrams are optional but must be **hand-drawn**

---

## **Question 1 – Pac-Man as an Object-Oriented Application (10 marks)**

a) Explain why **Pac-Man** is well suited to being designed as an object-oriented application.
(5 marks)

b) Identify **two objects** from the Pac-Man game and describe the **responsibility** of each object.
(5 marks)

---

## **Question 2 – Your Game as an Object-Oriented System (10 marks)**

a) Briefly describe your game project and its main objective.
(3 marks)

b) Identify **three objects** in your game and describe **what each object is responsible for**.
(7 marks)

---

## **Question 3 – Encapsulation (8 marks)**

a) Describe what **encapsulation** means in the context of an object-oriented game application.
(4 marks)

b) Using **one object from Pac-Man** and **one object from your own game**, explain:

* What data the object should control
* Why other objects should not directly access or modify that data
  (4 marks)

---

## **Question 4 – Abstraction (8 marks)**

a) Explain **abstraction** using **Pac-Man and Ghosts** as examples.
(4 marks)

b) In your own game, identify **two objects** that share similar behavior and explain how abstraction helps describe them at a higher level.
(4 marks)

---

## **Question 5 – Inheritance and Polymorphism (8 marks)**

a) Explain how **inheritance** could be used in Pac-Man or your game to reduce duplicated behavior.
(4 marks)

b) Describe an example of **polymorphism** that could exist in Pac-Man or your game.
(4 marks)

---

## **Question 6 – Aggregation and Interfaces (6 marks)**

a) Explain **aggregation** in a game application using Pac-Man or your game as an example.
(3 marks)

b) Describe the purpose of an **interface** in an object-oriented game design and give one example related to movement, collision, or interaction.
(3 marks)
